Hi, I'm Botond. I built Beamzy for a problem I kept running into myself.

I'm often away from my desk with only my MacBook, but the files I actually need are sitting on my home PC, and it's usually not small stuff either, more often than not it's large files. Cloud services turned out to be a bad fit for that: the good ones are expensive once you're moving real amounts of data regularly, and the free tiers are slow, capped, or both.

So I built Beamzy to just skip the middleman entirely. When both machines are home on the same network, files move directly between them at full local speed, no upload first and download later. When I'm away, a lightweight relay lets the two devices find each other over the internet, still without the files sitting on anyone's cloud storage in between.

That's really the whole idea: direct, fast, and free, without a subscription standing between you and your own files.
