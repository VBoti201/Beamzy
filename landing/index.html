Why Beamzy

Not a UI trick. Just fast.

Everything is built around one idea: your files should move as directly as possible.

Finds your other device

Pair once with a short code, and on the same Wi-Fi or network Beamzy sees your other computer automatically from then on. No IP addresses to type in.

Works away from home too

The same pairing code also lets your two devices send files to each other over the internet, whenever they're not on the same network.

Drag, drop, done

Files, images, videos, even whole folders (zipped automatically). Drop them in and they're on the other machine in seconds.

You control access

Choose exactly which folders are shared, and set browse/upload/download permission per device.

Nothing touches the cloud

Files go straight between your own devices. No third-party storage, no accounts required.

Stays up to date

New versions install themselves in the background. Nothing to download and reinstall by hand.

Questions

Good to know

Everything you might wonder before downloading.

What is Beamzy?
A small app that sends files directly between your Mac and Windows machine, on your network or anywhere else, using a short pairing code. No cloud upload in between.
Do I need an account?
No. Just open the app on both devices, there's nothing to sign up for.
Does it work when I'm away from home?
Yes. Pair your two devices once with a short code shown in Settings, and they'll be able to reach each other over the internet too, not just on the same Wi-Fi.
Is it free?
Yes, completely free.
Is it open source?
Yes. The source is on GitHub, so anyone can check exactly what it does.
How do updates work?
Beamzy checks for new versions in the background and installs them automatically. Nothing to download by hand.
What are the system requirements?
Really just two devices. Any mix of Mac and Windows works, paired once with a code, whether they're on the same network or not.

Ready to send your first file?

Grab it for your Mac or Windows machine, it takes under a minute to set up.
