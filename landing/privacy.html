Privacy policy

Last updated: August 24, 2026

Beamzy is built to move files directly between your own devices, not through a cloud service that stores or reads them. This page explains the little data that does pass through the developer's infrastructure while making that possible.

What Beamzy never sees

File contents are never stored on or inspected by the relay service. When two devices are on the same network, files go directly between them and never touch the developer's servers at all. When devices connect remotely, file data is streamed through the relay in transit only, the same way a phone call passes through an exchange, and isn't logged or retained.

What is processed, and why

DataPurposeRetention
Pairing codeLets your two devices find each other on the relayHeld only while a device is connected

IP addressNeeded to route the connection; also used for basic rate-limiting against abuseNot persisted beyond the active connection
Download countsThe public counter on the websiteAggregate numbers only — no data about who downloaded

No accounts, no third parties

Beamzy doesn't ask you to create an account and doesn't share data with advertisers, analytics providers, or any other third party. There's no tracking beyond the aggregate download counter described above.

Your rights

Since almost nothing is retained beyond an active connection, there's very little to request access to or deletion of. If you have questions about what's processed or want something clarified or removed, contact the developer using the details below — this applies to residents of the EU/EEA under the GDPR and to users elsewhere alike.
